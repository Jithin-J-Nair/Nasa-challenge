# NASA Catalogue of Manmade Material on the Moon

Source PDF: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/final-catalogue-of-manmade-material-on-the-moon.pdf

This checked-in text cache is the table-shaped text extracted from NASA's 2012 PDF. `scripts/parse-moon-catalogue.mjs` reads this cache (or a locally supplied copy of the named PDF via `pdftotext`) and produces the browser data and method note. Locations below intentionally preserve the catalogue's wording; blank, unknown, crashed, and unconfirmed locations remain blank rather than being guessed.
