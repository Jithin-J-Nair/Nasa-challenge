    02 / the source rule

    A story is an invitation.
    A source is a way back.

    42record links
    24checked dossiers

    Silent Fleet separates a human story moment from the NASA-checked record. The distinction is visible because the reader deserves to know which words are evidence and which words are reflection.

    NASA mission pages, catalogue records, image records, and dated status pages are the path back. When a record is incomplete, the archive says so rather than filling the silence.

    09 / evidence protocol

    Wonder needs
    an audit trail.

    The archive’s usefulness depends on keeping confidence and uncertainty visible at the same time.

    01Agency affiliation is not claim verification.

    Every visible statement needs its own source path.

    02A related image is not an object image.

    Each image is labelled as artefact, mission context, or placeholder.

    03A catalogue row is not a total mass.

    The Moon inventory preserves NASA’s own method.

    04Uncertainty remains part of the record.

    Withheld detail is more honest than a confident guess.
