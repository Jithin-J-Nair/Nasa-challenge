# Silent Fleet image and map credits

All runtime images are hosted locally. Dossier entries use an artefact-specific NASA image only when its NASA Image and Video Library or NASA mission record was identified during the claim-audit pass. Some entries use a mission-relevant image captured by or about the same mission; those are explicitly labelled as **not a photograph of the artefact**. Otherwise the site shows the clearly labelled `dossier-context-placeholder.svg`; it does **not** substitute an image of a different spacecraft.

## Maps and sharing image

- `textures/moon-lroc-color.jpg` — derived from the **CGI Moon Kit** color map. Credit: NASA/GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio, using Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter imagery and laser-altimeter data. Source: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720>.
- `textures/mars-usgs-mola-map.jpg` — compressed working texture derived from the **Simple Cylindrical Projection, Mars 2000 datum, MOLA background** map. Credit: NASA/USGS. Source context: <https://mars.nasa.gov/resources/6435/global-geologic-map-of-mars/>.
- `social/silent-fleet-og.jpg` — derivative social crop made for this site from the locally hosted CGI Moon Kit map above; credit NASA/GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio.

NASA states that its material is generally not subject to U.S. copyright, but individual assets can carry third-party or NASA/JPL credit requirements. This project retains the stated credit line, provides the original NASA source URL in every dossier, and makes no NASA endorsement claim.
